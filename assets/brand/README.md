## Usage

The horizontal lockup is the default. Keep clear space around it and place it on
a background with enough contrast to stay legible.

The Ilavrita name and marks are not covered by the AGPL licence that covers the
source. You may use them to refer to the project. You may not use them to imply
endorsement, or as the identity of a derived work — rename a fork. The trademark
policy is still under legal review.
